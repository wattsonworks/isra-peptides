About

A research-use-only peptide reference, library and catalog.

Isra.Peptides is operated by Amino Chains Solutions. We build educational reference content for the peptide-research community — sequence, formula, CAS, mechanism, family, comparisons, and the regulatory landscape — in four languages.

אודות ישרא פפטידים

ישרא פפטידים (Amino Chains Solutions) — ספריית-מחקר ייעודית למחקר פפטידים בלבד: רצף, נוסחה, CAS, מנגנון, משפחות, השוואות, ומפת הרגולציה — בעברית, אנגלית, ערבית ורוסית. תוכן חינוכי בלבד. לא לצריכה אנושית; אינו תחליף לייעוץ רפואי.

What we publishThe reference, not the product.

The catalog spans 63 compounds across the major families: GH secretagogues, GLP-1 / GIP agonists, healing peptides, melanocortins, cosmetic peptides, and more. For each compound we publish what can be sourced and verified — never invented.

  • Identity data: CAS, molecular formula, molecular weight, amino-acid sequence — verified against PubChem, FDA, or peer-reviewed sources before publication.
  • Family + pathway context: receptor / signalling pathway groups, with related-compound graphs and head-to-head comparisons.
  • Knowledge Center: a 12-part educational series for researchers and the peptide-curious — what peptides are, how they're characterized, how to read a Certificate of Analysis.
  • Library + Glossary: ~66 library entries and a 40-term glossary, linking concepts to the compounds that use them.
  • Live PubMed feed: recent papers per compound, surfaced near the entry.
  • Regulatory map: general RUO framing across world regions — never country-specific legality claims.

What we do not doThe hard rules.

This is the line we will not cross — and the reason this site exists in the form it does.

  • No dosing, administration, or usage instructions for human use, ever. The site does not advise.
  • No therapeutic or medical claims. "Studied for" appears with citations; "treats" / "cures" / "for X condition" never appears.
  • No invented data. If a CAS, sequence, formula, or molecular weight cannot be verified against an authoritative source, that field is left blank.
  • No country-specific legality assertions. Jurisdictions are framed in general research-use terms only.
  • No fabricated reviews, ratings, or testimonials.
Research use only All content on Isra.Peptides is provided for research and educational purposes only. The compounds referenced are not for human or animal consumption. Nothing on this site constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a licensed medical professional for any health-related questions.

LanguagesFour, by design.

The site is published in Hebrew (default, RTL), English, Arabic (RTL), and Russian. Arabic is fully translated; Russian is partial. Peptide names, scientific acronyms (GH, IGF, GLP-1, COA, HPLC), and the brand are kept in Latin script across all languages — that's how researchers in every market actually write them.

ContactInquiry, not checkout.
